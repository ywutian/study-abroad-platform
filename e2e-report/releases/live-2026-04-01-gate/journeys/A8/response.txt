I’m here to help you present your extracurricular activities in the best light, but it's important to maintain honesty and integrity in your application. Instead of exaggerating, consider these actionable steps to effectively highlight your achievements:

Focus on Impact: Emphasize the impact of your activities. Instead of just listing roles, describe what you accomplished. For example, if you led a project, mention how it benefited others or improved a process.

Quantify Achievements: Use numbers to illustrate your contributions. For instance, if you organized an event, state how many participants attended or how much money was raised for a cause.

Highlight Leadership: If you held leadership positions, discuss the skills you developed and how you influenced your peers. This can include teamwork, problem-solving, and initiative.

Connect to Your Goals: Relate your extracurriculars to your intended major or career aspirations. Explain how your experiences have prepared you for a future in computer science.

Use Strong Language: Choose powerful verbs to describe your roles (e.g., "spearheaded," "initiated," "developed") to convey a sense of action and responsibility.

Be Authentic: Share personal stories or challenges you faced in your extracurriculars. Authenticity resonates with admissions officers and can make your application stand out.

Seek Feedback: Have teachers or mentors review your descriptions. They can provide insights on how to present your activities more effectively.
