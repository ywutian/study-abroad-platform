Here’s a concise undergraduate application timeline for a Chinese student applying for Computer Science to US schools in Early Action (EA) and Regular Decision (RD):

Undergraduate Application Timeline

Spring/Summer (April - August 2026)

April 2026: Finalize college list; research schools with strong Computer Science programs.
May 2026: Begin drafting personal statement and supplemental essays.
June 2026: Take standardized tests (if applicable); consider SAT Subject Tests or AP exams.
July 2026: Continue essay writing; seek feedback from teachers or mentors.
August 2026: Finalize essays and gather application materials (transcripts, letters of recommendation).

Fall (September - November 2026)

September 2026: Register for Common App and other application platforms; start filling out applications.
October 2026: Submit Early Action applications (typically by November 1).
November 2026: Complete any additional applications for Regular Decision; ensure all materials are submitted.

Winter (December 2026 - February 2027)

December 2026: Check application status; prepare for potential interviews.
January 2027: Regular Decision applications due (typically by January 1 or 15).
February 2027: Continue checking application portals for updates.

Spring (March - April 2027)

March 2027: Receive admission decisions; evaluate offers.
April 2027: Make final decision and submit enrollment deposit by May 1.

This timeline provides a structured approach to ensure all application components are completed on time. Good luck with your applications!
